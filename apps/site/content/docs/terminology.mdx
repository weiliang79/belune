## The Name

**Belune** is a portmanteau of **beluga** — the small white whale — and **lune**, French for *moon*. The mark draws the name literally: a beluga nestled in a crescent moon.

## The Mark and Palette

The accent color splits by theme rather than staying fixed: **indigo (`#4249BD`)** in light mode, **violet (`#7C3AED`)** in dark mode. Dark is the default — Belune leans into the nocturnal, moonlit half of its own name, and the landing page's hero glow is the same violet rendered as a soft radial gradient.

The voice is deliberately **ease-forward** rather than enterprise-formal ("Deploy like the cloud," not "Enterprise-grade self-hosted PaaS orchestration platform") — the tagline drops "self-hosted" and "PaaS" on purpose so it reads the same regardless of how the project's business model develops.
